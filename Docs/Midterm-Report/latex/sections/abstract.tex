\begin{abstract}
Monocular depth models such as Depth Anything V2 return a depth value for every pixel and no indication of which values to trust. We are training a small convolutional head that reads the frozen depth model's decoder features, its predicted depth, the temporal spread of depth across neighboring frames, and two radar channels, and outputs per cell the probability that the depth error exceeds a fixed threshold. Labels come from lidar projected into the camera image, so no hand annotation is needed. At the midpoint of the project, a cache of head inputs holds 73\% of the nuScenes trainval camera images, and ten heads are trained: a baseline, its repeat, and eight runs that change the inputs or the seed. No head has been scored yet, because the evaluation code is not finished.\footnote{\raggedright Code: \url{https://github.com/kgqiao/monocular-depth-confidence-estimation}} This report reviews the literature, describes what is built, reports the cache statistics and the losses of the ten runs, and sets out the remaining plan.
\end{abstract}
